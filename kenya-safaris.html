Best Time to Visit Kenya

Dry Season (June – October): Ideal for wildlife viewing – animals gather around water sources. The Great Migration is in full swing in the Mara (July–September).

Short Rains (November – December): Lush landscapes, fewer crowds, and excellent birdwatching.

Calving Season (January – February): Newborn wildebeest in the Mara – predator action is intense.

Long Rains (March – May): Low season with great rates; some camps close but scenery is vibrant.
