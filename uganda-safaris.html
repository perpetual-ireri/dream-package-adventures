Uganda – The Pearl of Africa

Bwindi gorilla trekking, Queen Elizabeth's tree-climbing lions, and the thundering Murchison Falls.

Top Uganda Destinations

Bwindi Impenetrable Forest

Home to over half of the world's mountain gorillas – a life-changing trekking experience.

Queen Elizabeth NP

Famous for tree-climbing lions, boat cruises on the Kazinga Channel, and diverse wildlife.

Murchison Falls

The world's most powerful waterfall – boat safaris and Nile river cruises.

Best Time to Visit Uganda

Dry seasons (June–August & December–February) are best for gorilla trekking – trails are less muddy. The rainy seasons (March–May & September–November) offer lush landscapes and fewer crowds.
