About Us

We are Dream Package Adventures — a Kenyan safari and travel company built by people who grew up on these trails.

Who We Are

A Kenyan Company, Rooted in Africa

We are a team of local guides, drivers, and travel planners based in Nairobi. Our job is simple to make your journey through Africa feel personal, unhurried, and unforgettable.

Local Knowledge, Personal Service

We've spent years on these roads. We know which park gates open at dawn, which lodges treat guests like family, and which river bend the elephants cross at dusk. That kind of knowledge doesn't come from a booking platform it comes from living here.

When you travel with us, you're not handed off to a third party. You speak to the same people who plan your trip and often guide it too. One team, from the first email to the last airport drop-off.

Our Story

How We Started

Dream Package Adventures began as a small Nairobi outfit with a single Land Cruiser, a well-worn road atlas, and a simple idea: that African travel should be led by Africans, told in our own voice, and shaped around the traveller not the itinerary template.

Our founders, a safari guide and a hotelier, had both spent years watching visitors move through Kenya on tight, packaged schedules. They wanted to build something slower and more honest. Small groups. Real conversations. Time to sit still and let the bush breathe.

Over the years we've grown new vehicles, more guides, expanded routes into Tanzania, Uganda, Botswana, and beyond. But the founding idea hasn't changed. We still run the company the same way: one guest at a time, one carefully planned journey, one honest conversation at the start.
