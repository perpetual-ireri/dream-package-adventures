About Dream Package Adventures

Born in Nairobi, rooted in Africa – we craft journeys that stay with you forever.

Our Story

Dream Package Adventures was founded by a team of Kenyan travel enthusiasts who believe that Africa's wild places are best experienced with local expertise. What started as a small safari outfitter in Nairobi has grown into a trusted partner for travelers from around the globe.

We've guided thousands of guests through the Maasai Mara, the Serengeti, the Okavango Delta, and beyond – always with a commitment to sustainable tourism and genuine hospitality.

Our Mission

To connect you with Africa's wild heart through carefully curated, authentic, and responsible travel experiences. We believe in creating memories that respect the land, its wildlife, and its people.

  • Personalized service from a local team
  • Fair pricing with no hidden fees
  • Support for community and conservation projects
